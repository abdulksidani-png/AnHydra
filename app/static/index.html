ESM-2 turns the sequence into 480 numbers that capture its character. A classifier we trained on 3,151 proteins reads those numbers.

You get a score and the reasons

How much this protein looks like the families that survive total drying — plus the biophysical signatures behind the call, written out in plain language.

Why it is worth trusting

Tardigrades survive losing essentially all their water. A handful of protein families — CAHS in tardigrades, LEA and dehydrins in plants — do that work by turning the cell's interior into glass. Finding more of them is a real screening problem, and this is a real screen for it.
